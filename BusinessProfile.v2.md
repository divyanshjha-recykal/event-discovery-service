## Technology

- Hardware: Reklaim PRO, ACE, Mini, Neo, FastScan; modular form factor from 1 sq ft to 40 sq ft
- Compute: on-device edge AI on NVIDIA Jetson modules (Nano, Orin, Xavier); no cloud dependency for recognition
- Model: CNN plus attention/transformer architecture; 5-level taxonomy (material, form, rigidity, condition, brand); built with Google under a Responsible AI framework
- Sensing: fused RGB, time-of-flight depth and NIR imaging, with QR and telemetry data
- Training data: 2M+ labelled waste images across 50+ Indian locations, including crushed, wet, dirty and label-removed items
- Detection accuracy: >90% material detection across 200+ classes
- Contamination reduction from the model: ~60%
- Latency: <20 ms edge inference (raw model); <120 ms for full on-device recognition, contamination detection and validation
- Model optimisation: 60% smaller via quantisation; deployed with TensorRT
- Environmental resilience: solar-ready; operates from -10°C to 60°C; proven at 11,750 ft altitude (Char Dham)
- Language support: English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, Marathi; configurable per deployment
- Accessibility: audio prompts, icons and minimal-text UI for low-literacy users
- Fraud prevention: multi-factor verification; secure cloud telemetry; full audit trails
- Patent granted: 1 — "A Method and System for Automated Waste Counting, Identification, Classification, and Sorting with Artificial Intelligence", Indian Patent Office, granted 17 June 2026
- Patents published, not granted: 5

# What a good run looks like

A verified grounding set for Opportunity Radar, built from `BusinessProfile.md`
plus live web search and page-by-page verification, 20 September 2026.

This exists because "the pool looks better" is not a measurement. Every
judgement about whether the pipeline was improving has been made by eye against
no reference. This is the reference.

**Every positive below was fetched and read.** Nothing is listed on the strength
of a search snippet. Verification removed three candidates that looked strong
and were not — recorded in the negatives, because a reference set that contains
false positives punishes the pipeline for being right.

Primary market is **India**. UK, Bhutan and Mauritius are secondary and weighted
lower; global programmes count only where an Indian company can actually enter.

---

## 1. The axes

An opportunity is organised around one of six things. Retearn qualifies on all
six. A run that searches one axis can reach one sixth of the field, however good
its wording.

| Axis | What the programme is organised around | Retearn's qualifying facts |
|---|---|---|
| **Sector** | the industry served | circular economy, waste-tech, recycling, EPR |
| **Entrant type** | what kind of organisation applies | startup (incorporated 2024), bootstrapped, 38 employees, ₹4.76 cr FY25-26 |
| **Capability** | the technology itself | edge AI on Jetson, CNN + attention/transformer, RGB/ToF/NIR fusion, <120 ms on-device recognition, 2M+ labelled images, 1 granted patent |
| **Impact** | what it measurably changes | 38+ MT CO2e, 90% recycling rate, contamination 15% → <1%, 12,000+ informal workers, all-women field team |
| **Market** | where it operates | **India (primary)**, Bhutan, Mauritius, UK |
| **Function** | the discipline practised | hardware and product engineering, applied machine learning |

### The rule that governs all of them

**Search the technology, never the product.**

`Reklaim PRO`, `reverse vending machine`, `deposit return system` are product
names and categories. Confirmed by live search: no Indian conference, expo or
award is organised around reverse vending or DRS. Searched directly they return
vendors and explainer articles.

What programmes *are* organised around is the engineering underneath — edge
inference, computer vision, material classification, sensor fusion, low-power
embedded compute. Different bodies name those, and the profile's Technology
section is where they come from.

## 6. How to use this

**Recall check.** After a run, count how many of P1–P8 appear anywhere in the
search **pool** — not the results, the pool. That separates a retrieval failure
from a ranking failure, a distinction every debugging session so far has had to
guess at.

**Precision check.** Count how many of N1–N16 reached the pool, and where the
ranker placed them. Any negative ranked above a positive is a ranking failure
with a named cause.

**Eligibility check.** The verdict column is the expected answer from
`evaluate()`. N1, N2, N3 and N14 should come back *not eligible* with the
reason given. C1 should come back *unclear* on independence — if it returns a
confident yes or no there, the eligibility stage is over-reaching.

**Regression gate.** Before and after any prompt change, replay the same banked
pool and compare recall and precision. A change that improves neither is not an
improvement, whatever it looks like by eye.

**What it is not.** Not exhaustive, not a target to optimise against. A run that
finds something not listed here has not failed. It is a floor: a run that cannot
reach P1–P8 is not reaching much.

Assembled 20 September 2026. Every positive and every ★ negative was fetched and

Dates and open/closed states were correct at the time of writing and will drift.

AI's *innovation grants* are therefore out of scope even though its *workshop*
